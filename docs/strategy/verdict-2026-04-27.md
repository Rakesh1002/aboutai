# The AI Daily — Skeptical Operator Verdict

**Original kill memo:** 2026-04-27  
**Current refresh:** 2026-05-24  
**Decision:** **NO-GO** for the May 26 public launch; **conditional GO** only for a controlled validation sprint.

---

## Verdict

The old maximalist `aboutai` Trust Engine thesis stays killed. The remaining viable wedge is narrow:

> Operator-seat AI tool teardowns from 30 production stacks, written for Indian AI builders.

The daily brief may support habit and distribution, but it is not enough on its own. If the audience wants generic AI news, this venture should fold into AudioPod content marketing.

---

## Why No-Go Now

- The app had stale launch promises and a registered sample daily with fake-looking future news.
- The email copy promised a teardown date that had already passed.
- Bulk sending, Workflows/cron, and broadcast verification are not implemented end to end.
- The $10K MRR model depends on too many unproven assumptions at once: sponsor sell-through, paid conversion, daily cadence, teardown cadence, and edge ad inventory.
- Current web data validates the market, but not this operator's distribution or cadence.

---

## Conditional Go Criteria

Public sending is allowed only after:

- 4 daily issues are banked.
- 2 Friday teardowns are banked.
- Signup, confirm, unsubscribe, and one test broadcast are verified.
- Public app copy says validation list, not launch date.
- No placeholder/fake news is registered as published content.

---

## 30-Day Decision Gate

Continue only if the sprint produces:

- 12+ dailies.
- 4 teardowns.
- 500-1,000 subscribers.
- 40%+ open rate.
- 1 sponsor conversation or paid pilot.

The decisive qualitative signal is whether operator-seat teardowns outperform generic AI-news content in replies, shares, and conversions.
