## Trends & tailwinds
- **Tailwind:** AI-newsletter category is proven and monetizable; Indian AI ecosystem signal is real (e.g., Sarvam raising near a ~$1.5B valuation per [NDTV/Bloomberg](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/india-ai-startup-sarvam-raises-funds-at-1-5-billion-valuation-11305042)).
- **Headwind:** Google AI Overviews and zero-click search erode SEO-dependent publishers ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/impact-of-ai-overviews-how-publishers-need-to-adapt/556843/)) — favors owned-audience email over SEO.
- **Pricing reality:** sub-5K newsletters earn ~$50-$250 per placement; 5K-50K earn ~$500-$3,000 ([beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/newsletter-sponsorship-cost)) — do not assume aggressive week-one revenue.
