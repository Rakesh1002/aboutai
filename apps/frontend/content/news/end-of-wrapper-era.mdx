# The End of the Wrapper Era

<Callout type="info">
This is the aboutai manifesto—our declaration of what we believe the AI tool ecosystem needs, and what we're building to address it.
</Callout>

## The Problem We Saw

Between 2022 and 2024, something remarkable happened. The barriers to building "AI tools" collapsed. Anyone with an API key and a weekend could ship a product.

The result? An explosion of tools. Directories swelled to 5,000+ listings. Newsletters breathlessly covered every launch. Venture capital flowed.

But here's what nobody was asking: **Does this actually work?**

## The Wrapper Epidemic

We started digging. What we found was sobering.

Of the 500+ tools we analyzed in our first audit:

- **47%** were thin UI wrappers around ChatGPT with no proprietary value
- **23%** had broken or abandoned since their Product Hunt launch
- **68%** made claims that failed basic functional testing

The "AI tool" directories weren't directories of AI tools. They were directories of *landing pages*.

## The Trust Gap

This matters because real decisions are being made based on these tools:

- CTOs evaluating $50,000 enterprise contracts
- Solo founders betting their runway on automation promises
- Legal teams assessing compliance risks
- Healthcare organizations considering patient-facing AI

They're all asking the same question: **How do I know this actually works?**

And the answer from the current ecosystem is: You don't. You trust the marketing.

## What We're Building

aboutai is not another directory. It's a **Trust Engine**.

### Agentic Vetting

We deploy autonomous AI agents to test every tool we list. These agents:

1. **Detect wrappers** — Analyzing tech stacks, API dependencies, and latency patterns
2. **Functional test** — Running real tasks and measuring actual outputs
3. **Grade quality** — Using separate "grader" LLMs to evaluate for hallucination
4. **Monitor health** — Continuous checks for abandoned or broken tools

### The Trust Score

Every tool receives a composite Trust Score (0-100) based on:

| Component | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Proprietary Tech | 30% | Is this more than a wrapper? |
| Reliability | 40% | Does it actually work? |
| Transparency | 15% | Is the model/pricing clear? |
| Liveness | 15% | Is it actively maintained? |

### Radical Transparency

We publish the evidence. On every tool page, you can see:

- The exact prompts we used to test
- The responses we received
- The grader's evaluation
- Historical score changes

If we're wrong, you can see *why* we might be wrong.

## The Path Forward

The AI hype cycle is ending. What comes next is the **deployment phase**—where the question shifts from "what's possible?" to "what's reliable?"

We believe the winners of this phase will be:

1. **Tools with proprietary depth** — Not wrappers, but genuine innovation
2. **Vertical specialists** — AI for agriculture, not "AI for everything"
3. **Transparent operators** — Who publish their training data and benchmarks

And we believe there's a role for a media entity that holds this ecosystem accountable.
